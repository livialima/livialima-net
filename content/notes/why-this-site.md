I moved this site from MkDocs to Hugo because I wanted something lighter and easier to shape into a portfolio plus a place for evergreen notes.

The goals:

- A small, honest portfolio of the things I've built and maintain.
- A place to keep notes I can link to and search later, without the pressure of a "blog".
- A workflow I can keep up with: write markdown, commit, push, done.

If you're reading this, it's working. Feel free to delete or rewrite this note.
